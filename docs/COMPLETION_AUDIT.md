## Provenance and current acceptance gates

[The reader audit](../experiments/results/epoch-system-pipe-timestamps-20261023/reader-boundary-audit.json) records the distinction.

Modern campaigns write durable prelaunch source/config/binary/tool/host metadata and verify the immutable executable afterward. Earlier failed batch-load campaigns lacked a terminal manifest; their retrospective provenance states that limitation instead of inventing prelaunch proof. The original fault ENOSPC attempt, early system pilot, possible arrival coupling, unvalidated packet classifier, weak controls and discarded observation contracts stay identifiable and excluded where appropriate.
