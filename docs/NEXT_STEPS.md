Further work is optional and should begin with its own threat model and acceptance criteria:

1. **Isolated ORAM timing replication completed for two- and eight-address workloads, with frequency-only, operation-mix, address-prior and profile-only orientation follow-ups.** All 30-trial studies passed plaintext/encrypt-only positive controls. On the skewed 50/50 eight-address data, temporal RF AUC was 0.507 [0.496, 0.517] and frequency-only AUC was 0.498 [0.488, 0.508]. On matched skewed schedules with 90% reads, temporal RF AUC was 0.497 [0.490, 0.505]; a fresh-seed 10%-read run measured 0.496 [0.487, 0.505]. A separate uniform eight-address distribution with a 50/50 operation mix measured temporal RF AUC 0.493 [0.482, 0.503] and frequency-only AUC 0.505 [0.494, 0.515]. Profile-only direction selection measured ORAM AUC 0.499 [0.491, 0.507], 0.507 [0.497, 0.517], and 0.494 [0.483, 0.504] on those read-heavy, write-heavy and uniform workloads; intervals include chance and accuracy remains below the profile-majority baseline. See [skewed 50/50](ORAM_EIGHT_ADDRESS.md), [attack families](ORAM_ATTACK_FAMILIES.md), [orientation-selection results](ORAM_FREQUENCY_ORIENTATION.md), [read-heavy](ORAM_READ_HEAVY.md), [write-heavy](ORAM_WRITE_HEAVY.md), and [uniform addresses](ORAM_UNIFORM.md). Other distributions and attack families remain open; these near-chance results are not a security proof.
2. **Synthetic presence sensitivity completed:** eight scenarios test noisy semantic beacons, enrollment churn, missed cover observations, and changing populations over 240 sessions/scenario. The combined adverse synthetic case still enabled 79.4% owner recovery (95% whole-session bootstrap interval 73.3–85.0%) against 11.5% population-adjusted random accuracy. This is not real network evidence or differential privacy; see [the study](PRESENCE_SENSITIVITY.md). Real-network validation is data-dependent: no consented capture/access was available, so none was collected.
3. **Bounded human-query corpus evaluation completed:** the pinned MIMICS-Click query text drove the existing constrained-GGM SSE evaluator over 30 whole-query split trials. Exact-count and unpadded positive controls succeeded; public-bound padding reduced the count-volume attack to profile-majority levels on the 12.2%-coverage subset. See [corpus study and terms](HUMAN_QUERY_EVALUATION.md) and [aggregate output](../experiments/results/human-query-20261024/). MIMICS has no query arrival frequencies or temporal histories, its README has ambiguous licensing language, and this result is not representative of Bing traffic. Broader licensed trace access/query-prior drift remains open.
4. **Local batcher and full-path offer studies completed; deployment capacity/cost remain open.** The component sweep covers 100 epochs at populations 1/8/32/64. Full-path studies include 64 offers and a preregistered extension with 128 offers at 150 ms and 256 offers at 25 ms, each over 30 fresh processes/profile with actual PIR/WASM/ORAM work. The 128-offer schedule admitted all offers; the 256-offer schedule dropped 74.22% and missed the offered-load SLO for 99.22%. See [batcher results](POPULATION_SWEEP.md), [64-offer results](RUNTIME_POPULATION.md), and [extended-offer results](RUNTIME_POPULATION_EXTENDED.md). These are single-host, single-producer schedules, not remote capacity, multi-host population, long-term traffic tails, or provider-calibrated costs. Those remain open.
5. **Request-level and bounded-user autoscaler samplers implemented conditionally:** the finite-rational capped geometric mechanism reports a composed `(epsilon, delta)` bound for the fixed-horizon count/event observer under independent hidden uniform bits. Exact-rational tests enumerate the capped one-function request-level distribution for three parameter sets and all nonnegative pool shifts up to the user cap in three bounded-user parameter sets. These checks support the per-release bounds for those settings, but do not prove the coupling bound or composition for arbitrary parameters. A source-level sampler review found and fixed boolean acceptance in numeric configuration fields; it did not find a discrepancy in the reviewed sampler path. See [the sampler audit](SAMPLER_AUDIT.md). External cryptographic review, authenticated identity integration, accounting across sessions/deployments, and a complete runtime transcript remain. Optional per-user clipping requires stable non-spoofable IDs and caps the included scaling count across one horizon; the simulator does not authenticate identities, and the 30-trial synthetic cap-2 study shows higher simulated instance-seconds. Public-seed artifacts have no DP guarantee. A predeclared CSPRNG policy-interval attack passed all 28 naive controls; its interval features did not reliably improve paired protected accuracy, though four unadjusted protected results were above chance for hot-pool inference. See [the attack study](AUTOSCALER_INTERVAL_ATTACK.md). A fresh 30-trial OS-CSPRNG likelihood study passed all seven clear-count controls; the particle-filter attack scored 0.525–0.717 balanced accuracy over the tested workload/task cells, with four of seven unadjusted intervals above chance. See [the likelihood study](AUTOSCALER_LIKELIHOOD.md). This is workload-inference evidence, not a DP audit or privacy proof. Actual TEE/attestation and hardware side channels require supported hardware; recursive maps/native Circuit storage, DPF/native single-server runtime selection, and real worker-pool provisioning remain extensions.
6. Runtime signed-registry/PIR integration now carries verified 160-byte Ed25519 records through both local secure-PIR workers, with bounded database bootstrap and per-query exact pin checks. Its signer is ephemeral inside the trusted executor and does not establish external publisher identity; location digests are synthetic, and there is no persistent monotonic version anchor. Checkpoint and registry anchor CAS interfaces still lack an independent durable service. Durable invocation acknowledgments/side-effect ordering, persistent/backward-private SSE, signer custody, remote publication, and partial-catalog inclusion proofs remain before persistent application deployment.

The deterministic built-in demo remains a correctness/topology exercise. Its code/configuration can reveal the entire real/dummy/function schedule; encryption cannot conceal an identity already fixed by auxiliary knowledge. Experimental workload seeds/truth are separate private driver inputs under the declared attacker contracts, while mechanism parameters remain public. Accepted bounded experiments do not establish global indistinguishability or production security.
