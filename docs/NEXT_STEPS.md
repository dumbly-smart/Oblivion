Further work is optional and should begin with its own threat model and acceptance criteria:

1. **Isolated ORAM timing replication completed for two- and eight-address workloads, with a frequency-only attacker and read-heavy mix.** All 30-trial studies passed plaintext/encrypt-only positive controls. On the 50/50 eight-address data, temporal RF AUC was 0.507 [0.496, 0.517] and frequency-only AUC was 0.498 [0.488, 0.508]. On the same address schedule with 90% reads, temporal RF AUC was 0.497 [0.490, 0.505]. See [50/50 study](ORAM_EIGHT_ADDRESS.md), [attack family comparison](ORAM_ATTACK_FAMILIES.md), [read-heavy study](ORAM_READ_HEAVY.md) and [raw-hash verified traces](../experiments/results/oram-isolated-eight-address-20261024/). The initial unpaired two-address run is explicitly superseded. More operation mixes, address distributions and attack families remain open; these near-chance results are not a security proof.
2. **Synthetic presence sensitivity completed:** eight scenarios test noisy semantic beacons, enrollment churn, missed cover observations, and changing populations over 240 sessions/scenario. The combined adverse synthetic case still enabled 79.4% owner recovery (95% whole-session bootstrap interval 73.3–85.0%) against 11.5% population-adjusted random accuracy. This is not real network evidence or differential privacy; see [the study](PRESENCE_SENSITIVITY.md). Real-network validation remains future work.
3. **Bounded human-query corpus evaluation completed:** the pinned MIMICS-Click query text drove the existing constrained-GGM SSE evaluator over 30 whole-query split trials. Exact-count and unpadded positive controls succeeded; public-bound padding reduced the count-volume attack to profile-majority levels on the 12.2%-coverage subset. See [corpus study and terms](HUMAN_QUERY_EVALUATION.md) and [aggregate output](../experiments/results/human-query-20261024/). MIMICS has no query arrival frequencies or temporal histories, its README has ambiguous licensing language, and this result is not representative of Bing traffic. Broader licensed trace access/query-prior drift remains open.
4. **Local batcher and 64-offer full-path population studies completed; deployment capacity/cost remain open.** The component sweep covers 100 epochs at populations 1/8/32/64; the added full path measures 30 fresh processes/profile with 64 offers across underload and overload, checking real PIR/WASM/ORAM work. See [batcher results](POPULATION_SWEEP.md) and [full-path results](RUNTIME_POPULATION.md). These are single-host experiments at two schedules, not remote capacity, long-term traffic tails, or provider-calibrated costs. Broader populations, rates and deployment resources remain open.
5. **Event-level autoscaler DP analysis, seed-replay attacks, and epsilon sweep implemented conditionally:** `dp_laplace` specifies fixed-horizon add/remove-one-request sensitivity/composition and a post-processing boundary; its deterministic-public-seed artifacts have no formal DP guarantee. Centroid and forest seed-aware attacks passed naive positive controls; the paired four-budget study shows higher inference and lower simulated instance-seconds at ε=4, with higher SLO violations. Intervals are unadjusted and the cost is simulator-only. See [the study, attack results and sweep](AUTOSCALER_DP.md). Remaining work is stronger policy-aware inversion, a reviewed seed-hidden CSPRNG-backed mechanism, user-level contribution bounds, and evaluation on a complete runtime transcript. Actual TEE/attestation and hardware side channels require supported hardware; recursive maps/native Circuit storage, DPF/native single-server runtime selection, and real worker-pool provisioning remain extensions.
6. Design persistent/backward-private SSE, independently protected checkpoint-anchor publication, acknowledgment/side-effect ordering and dynamic registry signing/version policy before persistent application deployment.

The deterministic built-in demo remains a correctness/topology exercise. Its code/configuration can reveal the entire real/dummy/function schedule; encryption cannot conceal an identity already fixed by auxiliary knowledge. Experimental workload seeds/truth are separate private driver inputs under the declared attacker contracts, while mechanism parameters remain public. Accepted bounded experiments do not establish global indistinguishability or production security.
